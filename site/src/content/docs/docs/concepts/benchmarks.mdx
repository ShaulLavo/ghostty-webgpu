## November 2026 five-way comparison

Measured 2026-11-24 with `ghostty-webgpu` 0.3.34 against xterm.js 6.0.0 (WebGL addon 0.19.0), ghostty-web 0.4.0, Wraith (`wraithterm`) 0.6.1, and Restty 0.3.0 in headed Chromium 153 on two machines: a Linux desktop (Wayland, RTX 3060 Ti, DPR 1) and an Apple M1 MacBook (AC power, ANGLE Metal, DPR 2).

Every terminal received identical bytes, a `100x30` grid, and JetBrains Mono 14 px, with final screen text verified across 17 workloads at both 1 and 16 simultaneous terminals (34 cells per machine).

| Metric                                           | Linux PC                                     | Apple M1 Mac                               |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Whole-browser CPU vs. best competitor            | 26 wins, 5 even, 3 losses (median **0.71x**) | 24 wins, 9 even, 1 loss (median **0.77x**) |
| Mac kernel CPU energy (`RUSAGE_INFO_V6`)         | n/a                                          | 20 wins, 4 even, 10 losses                 |
| Write acknowledgement p95 (>10% & >0.5 ms loss)  | 4 losses (all heavy 16-terminal parsing)     | 0 losses                                   |
| Cold download (main entry, auto backend)         | **269.2 KiB** brotli                         | **269.2 KiB** brotli                       |
| Cold load to first paint (unthrottled / Fast 4G) | 80 ms / 823 ms                               | 101 ms / 861 ms                            |

The largest CPU leads show up on dense 16-terminal streams (`rolling-unicode-logs` `0.47x` PC / `0.50x` Mac), `sparse-edits` at 16 terminals (`0.45x` PC / `0.60x` Mac), `repeated-ascii` (`0.20x` PC / `0.35x` Mac), and `write-queue-overload` (`0.40x` PC / `0.51x` Mac, where `ghostty-web` 0.4.0 crashes).

## Where competitors still win

<Aside type="note" title="Read the losses alongside the wins">

Every benchmark hides tradeoffs if you only look at medians. Keep these four losses in mind when choosing a library:

- **Download size and Fast 4G cold load:** xterm.js (`84.1 KiB` brotli) and ghostty-web (`146.1 KiB` brotli) ship less code and paint sooner on Fast 4G than `ghostty-webgpu` (`269.2 KiB` brotli). Wraith (`879.0 KiB`) and Restty (`559.3 KiB`) are larger.
- **Glyph churn (1 terminal PC):** Restty WebGL2 uses less CPU (`0.151 s` vs. `0.222 s`) because `ghostty-webgpu` currently rasterizes each unseen glyph twice to detect intrinsic color glyphs.
- **Full-screen redraw Mac energy:** Wraith WebGPU uses less Mac CPU energy on several full-screen redraw workloads (`opentui-plasma`, `ntcharts-heatmap`, `synchronized-frames`).
- **16-terminal write acknowledgement p95 on PC:** Wraith WebGPU acknowledges writes sooner on 16-terminal `ntcharts-heatmap` and `opentui-plasma` because it spawns one worker per terminal, whereas `TerminalWorkerPool` caps workers at 4 to save 35% to 60% whole-browser CPU.

</Aside>
