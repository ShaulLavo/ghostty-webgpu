## Things to try

- **Edit in place:** use Left/Right arrows, Backspace, and Home/End before pressing Enter.
- **Browse history:** press Up and Down arrows to recall previous lines, or `Ctrl+C` to cancel the current line.
- **Select and copy:** drag across text to select, then press `Cmd+C` (macOS) or `Ctrl+Shift+C` (Linux/Windows).
- **Zoom font size:** press `Cmd+=` / `Cmd+-` / `Cmd+0` (or `Ctrl` equivalents on Linux/Windows) and watch the cell grid refit cleanly.
