# Next steps after the reference study

Status: Approved. Date: 2026-10-27. Source: [the study](README.md), merged at `d2ed8989a`.

This is the execution plan for everything the study found. Each item has an ID, a gate and a done condition. An item is done when its PR is merged, or when its experiment has a keep or reject decision written in `.internal/`. Experiment IDs (E1–E8) and ranked items (1–18) refer to the study's [ranked plan](README.md#ranked-improvement-plan) and [experiment briefs](README.md#experiment-briefs). Where an item says "per E*n*", the study's full brief is part of its contract. [Coverage](#coverage) maps every study recommendation and lane finding to an item here or to a recorded deferral.

## Constraints

These come from the study and apply to every item. Changing one needs new evidence and an ADR.

- Keep the unpatched pinned Ghostty core and the separate bridge (ADR 001). No patched Ghostty API.
- Keep the instanced WebGPU renderer (ADR 002) and the paged atlas allocator.
- Keep Canvas per-glyph painting in the Canvas backend; word-run replay already failed on contextual alternates.
- No full-viewport frame rebuilding, full-buffer uploads, or fixed clear-and-rebuild atlases.
- Browser rasterization stays the default. Any WASM font stack is opt-in.

## Ground rules

- **Baseline first, then change.** Every performance item gets a same-session, counterbalanced before/after pair on hardware with the existing `bench:renderer` / `bench:compare` tooling and the study's workloads (`/work/reports/ghostty-webgpu-reference-study-2026-10-27/bench/workloads/`).
- **Equivalent behavior is a prerequisite.** Every performance PR shows equal text and pixels (including wide, combining, overhanging and color glyphs), equal parsed input, unchanged public acknowledgements and frame-observer behavior, and the same required visible output. Record parsed, submitted and presented counts separately. Fewer submissions or presentations are allowed when removing them is the change's stated purpose (F2's redundant frames, F8's offscreen panes, coalescing); any other drop in work is a failure.
- **Endpoints are separate facts.** Write acknowledgement, frame submission, GPU queue completion and presentation are different measurements. Presentation means a Chrome `AnimationFrame::Presentation` trace event joined to our frame by command or frame identity. Screenshots prove content, not timing.
- **Byte replay is not app responsiveness.** Replayed workloads measure the terminal only. They exclude producer work such as Go/WASM generation, polling and image encoding, so terminal results never stand in for a live application's responsiveness.
- **Shared experiment defaults.** Unless an item says otherwise, performance runs use both hosts; WebGPU and WebGL2; 200×50 and 80×24 at DPR 2; 1 and 16 terminals; and the M1 workloads. Capability items (C1–C7) verify correctness in the browser test project on Chromium plus headed screenshots on the PC and the Mac, and run any performance gate at these defaults.
- **Two hardware hosts, used in turns.**
  - PC: RTX 3060 Ti, Linux. WebGL2 is the default backend there.
  - M1 Mac: WebGPU is the default backend there.
  - Each result names its host, browser, adapter and source SHAs.
- **Check VRAM before every headed window on the PC.** During the study, Hyprland's GPU memory grew to 6.96 GiB with many headed benchmark windows, and WebGPU device creation failed. Check `nvidia-smi` first and stop if free VRAM is under 2 GiB. Keep one headed benchmark browser open at a time.
- **One PR per item**, each with an independent reviewer that isn't its author. Changes touching the same files land in the order under [Dependencies](#dependencies-and-order).

- **X1 production** starts from merged F3 (#16) and C02 only. Do not carry C01's pipelines or C03's encoder into production. The shared drawing surface is a changed-premise experiment, not another encoder-sharing trial.
- **X7 production** step 1 is merged in #34. The open stack continues with #35 → #36. Its remaining steps stay in the [production migration note](../coherent-publication.md#production-migration).
- **X8** uses M2's timestamp-query baseline. Measure GPU time before selecting any draw variant.

## Decisions

- **F9 is rejected.** Both GPU vertex shaders already collapse blank glyph quads to a point, so the explicit flag check removed no covered fragments ([note](../blank-glyph-quads.md)).
- **X1 steps 1 and 3 are rejected; step 2 is kept for production extraction.** Reuse C02's atlas and A22's surviving shared CPU glyph-cache implementation and controls. Do not rerun pipeline or encoder sharing. The shared drawing surface is now running because C04 changed the premise with a many-canvas cost measurement (#28).
- **X2 and X4/X4b are rejected.** Their measured gates and retained controls are in the [glyph-batching note](../glyph-batching-experiment.md), [worker-pool note](../worker-pool-experiment.md), and [binary-delta note](../worker-binary-deltas.md).
- **X5 is deferred to Phase 3, X6 is not adopted, and C2 stays browser-rasterized.** M3 found no isolated compact-record, general-row-map, or in-WASM font-path win ([comparison](lanes/09-wraith-nm-comparison.md)).
- **X7 is KEEP with a production stack.** The accepted design preserves independent subscriber histories and accepts the documented recovery tradeoff. Step 1 is merged in #34. Steps 2 and 3 are open in #35 and #36 ([decision](../coherent-publication.md#decision)).
- **C4 is blocked** on the upstream Ghostty `f9ab34f` resize regression.
- **C5 is rejected.** Adaptive idle-immediate presentation misses the main-entry latency gate. Worker presentation is unresolved because the trace lacks a causal OffscreenCanvas frame join. The production scheduler stays on RAF ([experiment and baseline-gap finding](../idle-immediate-experiment.md)).
- **Ligatures (C2) and BiDi (C3) run after Phase 2** (owner left it to the coordinator, 2026-10-27). Neither is a gap against the ghostty-web baseline, and Phases 1 and 2 address measured costs.
