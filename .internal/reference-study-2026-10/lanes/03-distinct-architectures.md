## Comparison baseline: ours at `26d389150`

- Unpatched official libghostty-vt, separate bridge wasm importing its memory, public C ABI. The bridge exports callbacks and builds retained viewport data. - GPU records are 16 floats/64 B per cell plus 24 floats/96 B per glyph. Both have persistent full-viewport backing allocations. At 200×50 that is 1,600,000 B of record capacity, before cached inputs/rendered cells, glyph bitmaps, core memory, and GPU copies. ### Frames, GPU and scheduling

- Each `restty_render_update` copies **all visible rows and cells** out of Ghostty RenderState into structure-of-arrays codepoints/styles/colors/wide/graphemes/link buffers. JS typed arrays view those buffers without another extraction copy, but that does not undo the full native extraction or later JS/GPU copies. No dirty-row filter or scroll ring offset in this function. `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 wasm/src/restty.zig:845]` `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 wasm/src/restty.zig:880]` `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/wasm/runtime/render-state.ts:18]`
- On a rendered frame, TS again walks every row/cell, builds JS number arrays for backgrounds/decorations/glyphs grouped by font/filter, converts each to Float32Array, and uploads complete batches. No retained per-row GPU record mapping or sparse update threshold found in this path. Scrolling therefore rebuilds the visible geometry, even if shapes/glyph bitmaps hit caches. `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/runtime/create-runtime/render-tick-webgpu-cell-pass.ts:276]` `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/runtime/create-runtime/render-tick-webgpu-draw-pass.ts:174]` `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/runtime/create-runtime/render-tick-webgpu-draw-pass.ts:208]`
- Instanced quads, not fragment-shader cell lookup. Base order is background/selection rectangles, image underlays, decorations/procedural rectangles, main glyphs, image overlays, top rectangles, overlay glyphs. Draw count depends on nonempty groups/font/filter batches, rather than being fixed at two. Custom post-shader stages each add a render pass and fullscreen quad; they are optional and should be disabled for a text baseline. `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/runtime/create-runtime/render-tick-webgpu-draw-pass.ts:353]` `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/runtime/create-runtime/render-tick-webgpu-draw-pass.ts:386]` `[src wiedymi/restty@3db45b0 src/runtime/create-runtime/render-tick-webgpu-draw-pass.ts:399]`
- Loop schedules RAF unconditionally, even when idle/paused, but only rebuilds when `needsRender`, resize activity, blink, or image animation warrants it. Hidden documents have a lower render budget.
