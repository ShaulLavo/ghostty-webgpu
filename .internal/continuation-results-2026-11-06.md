# Continuation results, November 6, 2026

Plan 005 remains Approved. Its bounded record-construction attribution did not justify requesting a compact-layout design. This is not a formal rejection against the 20% CPU gate. An equal-output, ownership-matched comparison that identifies a record-attributable opportunity remains necessary. The external worker comparison also needs painting qualification and matched runtime, device, and worker counts before design.

Plan 006 evaluated official Ghostty `2c76f29972dad21d3f2cc0efde2517a6fa8fed56`. The original native C resize regression failed on that candidate and passed on the shipped pin. The candidate is rejected; the upstream pin and WASM remain unchanged. Broad candidate qualification was not run.

The seven conditional items in plans 011 and 013 remain Approved, with their implementation triggers unmet:

- A named consumer requiring many busy Linux WebGPU panes for a shared drawing surface.
- Measured GPU dominance after X8 and X5 for fullscreen lookup or MSDF.
- An explicit consumer or use case for images and named-buffer transport.
- A current Canvas performance target for a Gespenst comparison.
- An existing WASM font backend for persistent font assets and startup measurements.
- Observed bridge fragility for additional ABI introspection or callback machinery. The current bridge already reads the ABI manifest and installs callbacks.
- A real non-VT consumer for public direct cell-grid ownership.

These findings do not change the historical decisions or authorize new implementations.
